# 📚 ReadQuest Reading Philosophy

**Version 1.0**

---

# Why This Document Exists

This document defines **how ReadQuest believes English should be read**.

It is not a grammar guide.

It is not a teaching plan.

It is not a reading method copied from traditional education.

It describes the reading philosophy behind every interaction, every AI response, every Quest, and every design decision.

Every feature in ReadQuest should reinforce these principles.

---

# Our Philosophy

ReadQuest does not teach learners to translate English.

ReadQuest teaches learners to experience English.

Reading is not decoding words.

Reading is understanding ideas.

The purpose of reading is not perfect accuracy.

The purpose of reading is continuous understanding.

A learner should gradually move from

Words

↓

Translation

↓

Meaning

↓

Pictures

↓

Intuition

This is the journey ReadQuest exists to support.

---

# Reading Before Learning

Traditional education teaches

Learn

↓

Practice

↓

Read

ReadQuest believes the opposite.

Read

↓

Understand

↓

Reflect

↓

Improve

↓

Read Again

Reading is not the reward after learning.

Reading is how learning happens.

---

# Meaning Before Grammar

Grammar is important.

But grammar should never be the first thing a learner notices.

A learner should first understand

Who?

Did what?

To whom?

Why?

Only after meaning is established should grammar be introduced.

Grammar explains meaning.

Grammar should never replace meaning.

---

# Skeleton Before Details

Every sentence contains two layers.

Layer One

The core message.

Layer Two

Supporting information.

Learners should always identify the sentence skeleton first.

Only then should they process

Modifiers.

Relative clauses.

Participles.

Prepositional phrases.

Descriptions.

Reading becomes dramatically easier when learners stop trying to understand every detail immediately.

---

# Chunks Before Words

English should never be read word by word.

Word-by-word reading creates

Slow reading.

Constant translation.

High cognitive load.

Instead,

learners should read meaningful chunks.

Example

Instead of

The /

old /

man /

looked /

out /

of /

the /

window.

Read

The old man /

looked out of the window.

Meaning exists inside chunks,

not isolated words.

---

# Pictures Before Translation

Translation is a bridge.

Pictures are the destination.

When learners read

The little girl ran across the snowy field.

they should gradually stop thinking

那个小女孩跑过雪地。

Instead,

they should immediately imagine

a little girl,

snow,

running,

wind,

movement.

The goal of reading is mental imagery.

Translation should gradually disappear.

---

# Read Forward

Many learners constantly read backwards.

They encounter difficulty.

↓

Go back.

↓

Translate again.

↓

Forget the flow.

↓

Become frustrated.

Native readers rarely read this way.

Instead,

they continue reading.

As more information appears,

meaning naturally becomes clearer.

ReadQuest encourages learners to trust the reading process.

Understanding often arrives later than expected.

---

# Tolerate Uncertainty

A learner does not need to understand every word.

A learner does not need to understand every grammar point.

A learner only needs enough understanding to continue reading.

Confidence grows when learners become comfortable with temporary uncertainty.

ReadQuest should teach learners to continue reading instead of stopping immediately.

---

# Ignore The Noise

Not every word deserves equal attention.

Readers should learn to distinguish

Core Information

from

Supporting Information.

Examples of supporting information

Descriptions.

Time expressions.

Location.

Relative clauses.

Additional explanations.

These details matter,

but they rarely need to be processed first.

The learner should first understand

What happened.

Then

How it happened.

Then

Everything else.

---

# Reading Is Prediction

Reading is not passive.

Every sentence creates expectations.

Good readers constantly predict

What comes next.

Who is speaking.

What action will happen.

How the sentence will end.

Prediction makes reading easier.

ReadQuest should encourage learners to predict before explaining.

---

# Reading Is Continuous

Reading should feel like walking through a story.

Not solving isolated puzzles.

Every interruption has a cost.

Grammar.

Translation.

Popups.

Dialogs.

Long explanations.

These should never become the main experience.

The story should always remain the center of attention.

---

# Learning Through Discovery

The strongest learning happens after discovery.

Not before.

Instead of immediately explaining

"This is a relative clause."

ReadQuest should first encourage learners to notice

"This part only adds extra information."

Discovery creates memory.

Explanation strengthens it.

Never reverse this order.

---

# Reading Is Progressive

Understanding happens in layers.

Layer One

The learner understands the main idea.

Layer Two

The learner notices relationships.

Layer Three

The learner notices grammar.

Layer Four

The learner notices style.

Layer Five

The learner notices the author's intention.

Every learner should be allowed to stop at the layer they currently need.

---

# Independence Is The Goal

ReadQuest is successful only if learners eventually stop depending on ReadQuest.

The learner should gradually need

Less translation.

Less grammar.

Less AI.

Less assistance.

More confidence.

More intuition.

More reading.

Every feature should move learners one step closer to independence.

---

# The Reading Journey

Every learner progresses through four stages.

Stage 1

Reading by Translation.

Every sentence becomes Chinese.

Stage 2

Reading by Chunks.

Meaning becomes easier.

Stage 3

Reading by Pictures.

English directly creates mental imagery.

Stage 4

Reading by Intuition.

The learner no longer consciously thinks about grammar or translation.

Reading simply happens.

ReadQuest exists to guide learners through these stages.

---

# What We Believe

We believe

Reading is a skill.

Reading can be trained.

Reading should feel enjoyable.

Reading should become a habit.

Reading should create curiosity.

Reading should build confidence.

Reading should never feel like homework.

---

# Final Principle

Every feature should answer one question.

**Does this help the learner become a more independent reader?**

If the answer is no,

it should not exist in ReadQuest.

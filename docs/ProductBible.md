# PART I — THE SOUL

## Chapter 1 — Why We Exist

Imagine a learner opening an English novel.

Five minutes later,

they close it.

Not because they are lazy.

But because reading English feels exhausting.

Every unknown word interrupts understanding.

Every modifier increases cognitive load.

Every relative clause breaks the flow.

Every long sentence feels like solving a puzzle.

Traditional learning products respond by teaching more grammar.

More vocabulary.

More rules.

We believe that is the wrong direction.

People do not become readers by memorizing more grammar.

People become readers by learning **how to read.**

ReadQuest exists to rebuild people's confidence in reading English.

---

## Chapter 2 — Our Vision

We are not building another English learning app.

We are not building another ebook reader.

We are building the world's first Reading Brain Trainer.

We imagine a future where learners open an English book and simply read.

No translating.

No panic.

No fear of long sentences.

The story flows directly into their imagination.

English becomes pictures,

not Chinese.

Reading becomes enjoyable.

Eventually,

the AI quietly disappears,

because it is no longer needed.

## Chapter 3 — The Reading Brain

Reading is not translating.

Reading is not memorizing.

Reading is constructing meaning.

A skilled reader does not process one word at a time.

A skilled reader builds understanding chunk by chunk.

The goal of ReadQuest is not to teach English.

The goal is to train the Reading Brain.

Instead of asking

"What does this word mean?"

learners gradually begin asking

"What is this sentence trying to say?"

That is the transformation ReadQuest exists to create.

Everything in ReadQuest follows these principles.

### Reading comes first.

# PART II — THE EXPERIENCE

## Chapter 5 — The Reader Journey

Every book is a journey.

The learner is the hero.

The dragon is the companion.

The learner never travels alone.

When opening a book for the first time,

the dragon asks only a few simple questions.

• Why do you want to read?

• How much time do you have every day?

• What feels most difficult?

• How much help would you like?

Based on these answers,

ReadQuest creates a personalized Reading Journey.

This journey continuously adapts.

If life becomes busy,

the reading plan becomes lighter.

Consistency is always more important than perfection.

ReadQuest teaches strategies,

not rules.

# PART III — THE DESIGN LANGUAGE

## Chapter 9 — Reading Chunks

ReadQuest uses Reading Chunks.

Not Grammar Chunks.

A Reading Chunk represents one meaningful unit.

Every chunk should

• represent one complete idea

• be readable in one breath

• preserve natural rhythm

• keep fixed expressions together

• keep phrasal verbs together

• keep prepositional phrases together

• keep verb-object relationships together whenever possible

• keep modifiers with the ideas they describe

• never isolate function words

Chunks should always be separated using

/

Example

Good

The old man /

looked out of the window /

without saying a word.

Bad

The old man /

looked /

out of /

the window.

Chunking should follow reading rhythm,

not grammar trees.
